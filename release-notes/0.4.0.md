Set shortcuts right from the cheat sheet, show them on screen for recordings, and see your month in Press59.

- **Edit from the cheat sheet:** hold Caps Lock, then click a key to give it the app you're in, another open app, or any app. Once it's open, you can type a key too.
- **Shortcuts on screen:** for screen recordings, streams and demos, a badge shows what each shortcut did and the keys that did it. Turn it on in the menu or in Settings.
- **Your Month in Press59:** a card of your month (app switches, top apps, streak) to post or share, with earlier months a click away. The counts stay on your Mac; turn counting off or reset it in Settings.
- **Keyboard layouts:** on Colemak, AZERTY or Dvorak, a shortcut is the letter the key types, and the cheat sheet shows your layout. In a non-Latin layout such as Russian, shortcuts use the letters of your Latin layout.
- **Your shortcuts:** hold Caps Lock on its own for half a second to see them all on a keyboard. The menu now points there instead of listing every shortcut.
