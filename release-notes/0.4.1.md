Fixes for Caps Lock getting stuck, and a way to quit when the menu bar icon is hidden.

- **Caps Lock no longer gets stuck:** after a shortcut took you to a password field, or while an app wasn't responding, Press59 could miss Caps Lock being released and treat every key as a shortcut. It now notices and lets go.
- **No stray letters:** holding a shortcut's key after letting go of Caps Lock no longer types it into the app that just opened.
- **Quit from Settings:** when macOS hides the menu bar icon, open Settings with Caps Lock + , and quit from the General tab.
