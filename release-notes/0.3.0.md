Press59 now moves windows too, and shows your shortcuts when you hold Caps Lock.

- **Move windows:** Caps Lock + ← → ↑ ↓ for halves, thirds and quarters. Caps Lock + Return fills the screen; press again to center.
- **Cheat sheet:** hold Caps Lock on its own to see your shortcuts.
- **Dock apps:** on first launch, Press59 suggests a letter for each app in your Dock. Pick the ones you want.
- **Guide:** "How Press59 Works" explains every key. It's in the menu and Settings.
- **Settings:** split into Shortcuts, General and About tabs.

Window moving can be turned off in Settings; Caps Lock + arrows then go out as ⌃⌥⇧⌘ + arrow, as before.
