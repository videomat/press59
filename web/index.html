Questions

Does it break Caps Lock?

No. Tap Caps Lock quickly on its own and capitals toggle as always. It only becomes Hyper while you hold it and press another key.

Why does it need Accessibility access?

To catch a shortcut before the app you’re typing in sees it — that’s what keeps a stray letter from being typed. Press59 never records or sends what you type.

Why isn’t it on the Mac App Store?

Apps that intercept keystrokes can’t run in the App Store sandbox, so Press59 is distributed directly from this site.

Can I use it alongside Raycast or Alfred?

Yes. Turn off any other Hyper key setting so only one app owns Caps Lock, then use Hyper shortcuts in those apps as usual.
